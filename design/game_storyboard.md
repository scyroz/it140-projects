# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game

## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:** Sci-Fi Space Station Survival

**Storyline:**
An Alien Parasite has infested the Command Bridge of an abandoned research station. Life support systems are failing, and the player must explore the interconnected station modules to collect six critical tools—a Data Drive, Plasma Cutter, Energy Shield, Space Suit, Oxygen Mask, and Ration Pack—before confronting the parasite in the Bridge to restore power and survive.

## Rooms
1. Central Hub (Start room)
2. Archives
3. Security Wing
4. Armory
5. Crew Quarters
6. Reactor Core
7. Mess Hall
8. Bridge (Villain room)

## Items
1. Data Drive (Archives)
2. Plasma Cutter (Security Wing)
3. Energy Shield (Armory)
4. Space Suit (Crew Quarters)
5. Oxygen Mask (Reactor Core)
6. Ration Pack (Mess Hall)

## Villain
Alien Parasite: A hostile alien organism nesting in the Bridge. Entering without all six items results in immediate game over.
