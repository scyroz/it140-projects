# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game

## Theme and Storyline
**Theme:** Medieval Castle Fantasy Adventure

**Storyline:** The dragon has taken over the dining room and your party guests will arrive in a matter of hours. You need to defeat the dragon before your guests arrive, but before you fight the dragon you will need to gather six essential relics and supplies scattered across the castle: a book from the library, armor from the bedroom, a helmet from the cellar, a sword from the dungeon, a shield from the gallery, and a peanut butter sandwich from the kitchen[cite: 1]. If you face the dragon unprepared without collecting all six items, you will be defeated immediately.
